Undergraduate Research

Cognitive Security Accountability, Fairness and Explainability (CAFE LAB)

2025-2026

LoRA fine-tuning is far cheaper than full fine-tuning. This project measures whether the malware explanations it produces are still good enough to use.

Outcome LoRA matched most of full fine-tuning's explanation quality while training faster and producing a much smaller model. The paper was accepted to IEEE Big Data 2025.

Project Overview

EMBER + SHAP + LLM Comparison Pipeline

Role: Undergraduate Researcher

I built and validated a malware explanation pipeline that puts parameter-efficient tuning (LoRA) head to head with full fine-tuning. It runs from data prep through generating the explanations, then scores each approach on quality against what it cost to train.

Contributions

Key Work Completed

  • Used the EMBER 2018 dataset with a LightGBM classifier and SHAP feature analysis.
  • Trained and compared multiple LoRA configurations against a full fine-tuned LLM baseline.
  • Generated human-readable malware explanations from model outputs and feature-importance signals.
  • Evaluated quality and efficiency trade-offs to identify practical deployment configurations.
  • Packaged scripts and setup flow for reproducible experimentation in the public repository.
