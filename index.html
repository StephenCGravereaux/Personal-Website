Overview

Professional Summary

Most of my work comes down to keeping systems running, keeping networks secure, and making sure people can actually get help when something breaks. In practice that has meant Linux and Windows administration, virtualization, routing and switching, and account support, usually in fast-moving environments.

  • Reliable operations. Systems and communication services that stay up. Uptime is the thing I optimize for first.
  • Security-minded by habit. Careful configuration, tight access control, and troubleshooting done methodically instead of fast.
  • Clear support and leadership. I train teams, explain things to users without the jargon, and follow problems through to a fix.
